## Purpose

Verify the original pixel Montezuma learner on one owned ACES H100 PCIe allocation. This operational site check grants no scientific, comparison or idea credit and admits no workers.

## Key hyperparameters

One policy, 128 environments, 128 rollout steps, four epochs, four minibatches, fifty observation warmup rollouts and one complete update. Networks and optimizer state remain float32; running statistics remain float64. Highest arithmetic precision and deterministic operations remain required.

## Code and config changes

The separate ACES profile requires driver 580.178.04 and recorded root-owned cached GRES and task/cgroup configuration. Ambiguity is saved and rejected before JAX. Slurm's CUDA selector is preserved until a fresh identity child proves the actual initialized CUDA UUID and PCI address. Full original learner and renderer checks then run in distinct fresh children; a final probe repeats identity, captures its actual mapped driver bytes, checks all dtypes and finite state, and performs one update. No full NVIDIA XML, process queries or unassigned device probes are permitted. Configured device restrictions are recorded; actual kernel enforcement is not independently attested.
