## What the Warp environment is

`exploration_platform/envs/antmaze/warp_antmaze.py` (`WarpAntMaze`), with the interface of `mjx_antmaze.JaxAntMaze`. Unless a row says otherwise:

1. **Model**: the XML of `am_common.build_antmaze_xml`, the same string the JAX implementation loads (Gymnasium Ant plus the merged wall boxes), 5 physics steps of 0.01 s per environment step.
2. **Integrator and solver**: `implicitfast`, Newton solver, pyramidal friction cones, at most 4 solver iterations and 8 line-search iterations per physics step (the model's `iterations` and `ls_iterations`). MuJoCo Warp ends the solver loop of a physics step when every environment has converged or reached the limit. Float32.
3. **Collision**: MuJoCo Warp's defaults (all-pairs broad phase with its plane, sphere and oriented-box filters). The two contact caps of the model (`max_geom_pairs`, `max_contact_points`) are read only by MJX's JAX implementation.
4. **Sizes**: one contact pool of 48 slots per environment shared by all environments (`naconmax` = 48 x batch; candidate geom pairs use a pool of the same size), and at most 160 constraint rows per environment (`njmax`).
5. **Overflow**: when a pool or the row limit is exceeded MuJoCo Warp drops the excess and goes on. The environment state keeps the largest number of contacts and of candidate pairs in the pool, the largest number of rows of one environment, and MuJoCo Warp's overflow flags per environment; `WarpAntMaze.raise_on_overflow` raises when any of them shows a drop. Every Warp cell below calls it, so a unit with dropped contacts ends with an error.
6. **Foreign call**: `mujoco.mjx.step` on a model put with `impl="warp"`, batched with `jax.vmap` over one axis, inside `jax.jit` and `jax.lax.scan`, graph mode `WARP` (Warp captures a CUDA graph per set of buffer addresses and replays it).

## The trainer's kind of program

One compiled function with the environment state and the observation donated, a `lax.scan` over 128 environment steps, the returned state fed to the next call, the step limit lowered to 50 so that every environment is reset inside every scan, and a 64-bit sum beside the 32-bit physics. The time per environment step is the median over the calls after the first two. The graphs captured are counted over the whole process (cells measured earlier in the same process captured graphs too), so the column shows whether the count still grows from call to call: it grows in the first two calls, by 1 per call with the scan not unrolled and by 16 per call with it unrolled 16 steps, and then stays. In graph mode `WARP_STAGED` the counters MuJoCo Warp returns (contacts, candidate pairs, rows, solver iterations) read 0 at every step, so that mode cannot report an overflow.
