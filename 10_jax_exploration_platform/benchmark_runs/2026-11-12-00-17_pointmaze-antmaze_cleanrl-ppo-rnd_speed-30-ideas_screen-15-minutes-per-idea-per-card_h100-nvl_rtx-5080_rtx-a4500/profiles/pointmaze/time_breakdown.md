# PointMaze Large: where the time of one training iteration goes

Generated by `make_tables.py` from `time_breakdown/*.json`. Learner: the study's (clipped PPO, 4 epochs x 4 minibatches, 128-step rollouts, random network distillation, episode log and coverage map on); minibatches are gathered one epoch at a time at 128 environments per policy.

- **Whole iteration:** the median over alternating rounds of chained calls with one wait at the end of a block, as a training loop runs.
- **Each piece:** its own compiled function that returns all it computes, median over calls with a wait after each. A piece timed alone pays its own start and wait, so the pieces add up to more than the iteration.
- **Share:** $\dfrac{\text{piece milliseconds}}{\text{whole-iteration milliseconds}} \times 100$, with the whole iteration timed one call at a time with a wait, as the pieces are.
- **Episode log and coverage map, in the iteration:** the iteration as the study runs it minus the iteration built without that part, in milliseconds (chained calls, same process, alternating rounds).
