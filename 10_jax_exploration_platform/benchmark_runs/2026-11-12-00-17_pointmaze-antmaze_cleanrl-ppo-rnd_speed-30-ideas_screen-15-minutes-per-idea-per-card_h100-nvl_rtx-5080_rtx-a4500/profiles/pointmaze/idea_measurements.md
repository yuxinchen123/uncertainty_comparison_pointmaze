# PointMaze Large: paired timings of the speed ideas

Generated by `make_tables.py` from `ideas/*.json`. All arms of a record are built in one process and timed in alternating rounds of chained iterations after two warm-up iterations each. The first arm is the reference.

- **Ratio:** per round, $\dfrac{\text{reference seconds per iteration}}{\text{arm seconds per iteration}}$, which is the arm's total steps per second relative to the reference; the table shows the median over the rounds, the least and the most, and in how many rounds the arm was faster.
- An idea is kept only when the ratio is at least 1.05.
