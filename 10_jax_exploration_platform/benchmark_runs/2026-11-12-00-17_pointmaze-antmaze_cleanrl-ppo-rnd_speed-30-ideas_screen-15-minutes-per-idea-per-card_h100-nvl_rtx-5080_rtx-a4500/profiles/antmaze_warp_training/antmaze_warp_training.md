# Training iteration with the Warp AntMaze physics (AntMaze U-maze)

Generated by `benchmarks/env/profile_antmaze_warp_training.py --render` from the records in `records/`; change the script, not this file. Times are Pacific, converted from machines running on Eastern.

## What was measured

`Runner.iterate` of the platform with the study's learner, read from `configs/experiments/antmaze_umaze_warp-physics_cleanrl-ppo-rnd_goal-termination_reward-arrival-plus-one_example_steps-204800.toml`: clipped PPO in 4 epochs of 4 minibatches, 128-step rollouts, random network distillation on the next state, the episode ending at the goal, the episode log and the coverage map on, MuJoCo Warp physics with a shared pool of 48 contact slots per environment. One process per cell. Define $c$ as the number of policies, $e$ as the environments per policy and $s$ as the median seconds per iteration over rounds of chained calls with one wait for the card per round (the first three calls, which include compilation, are left out).

- Total steps per second: $\dfrac{128 \times c \times e}{s}$, shown in millions.
- Steps per second per policy: $r = \dfrac{128 \times e}{s}$.
- Hours per million steps per policy: $\dfrac{10^{6}}{3600 \times r}$.
- Rollout share and update share: the median seconds of that piece compiled and timed alone (the rollout scan of 128 steps of policy forward and environment; the update with its shuffle, gather, sixteen gradients, clip and Adam), divided by $s$. A piece alone waits for the card after every call, and the rest of the iteration (value pass, distillation forward, statistics, advantages, episode log, coverage map) is not timed alone, so the two shares do not add up to 100%.
- Peak card memory: what the process held on the card after the timed rounds, as nvidia-smi reports it for the process. JAX does not hand memory back, so this is the most the iterations needed, with the memory JAX took but no longer uses included.
- Largest pool use: the larger of the contacts and the candidate pairs in the shared pool at any physics step, divided by the pool's slots. Nothing was dropped in any row shown.

## Episodes that end at the goal without a walk (H100 NVL, serval02 card 1)

The measured cells above counted episodes that ended at the goal after 15 iterations of an untrained policy. The start cell and the goal cell of the U-maze are 8 m apart with a wall cell between them (the way around is about 16 m), so these are not walks. `units/diagnose_arrivals_with_random_torques.py` stepped ants with random torques (standard normal, clipped to [-1, 1], fresh at every step), the episode ending at the goal or after 700 steps, with each implementation of the physics, and kept the torso's position and velocity of every step. The walls are 2 m high.

- MJX JAX: 15 of the 15 episodes that ended at the goal went through the wall cells between the start row and the goal row, above the wall: the largest torso height of these episodes was between 3.18 m and 15.83 m, and they ended at the goal after 133 to 688 steps.

In both implementations some ants rise above the walls and cross them; the records hold every such episode that ended at the goal (`records_arrivals/`). Nothing in the environment ends or discards an episode in which the torso is above the walls.
