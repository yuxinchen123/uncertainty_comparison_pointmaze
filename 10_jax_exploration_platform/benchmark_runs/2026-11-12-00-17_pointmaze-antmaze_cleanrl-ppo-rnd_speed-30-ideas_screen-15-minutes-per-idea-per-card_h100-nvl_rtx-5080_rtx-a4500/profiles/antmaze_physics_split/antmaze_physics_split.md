### What the settings rows say about the solver (estimates)

Define $T(i)$ as the milliseconds per environment step with $i$ solver iterations and $T_4$ as the time with 4 line-search iterations instead of 8, other settings at their defaults. All five quantities assume the time is a straight line in the number of iterations, and the pass without the loop comes from the 1-iteration model, whose physics is unstable (see the resets above), so read them as estimates.

- One solver iteration inside the loop: $u = \dfrac{T(4) - T(2)}{2}$.
- Everything outside the solver iterations: $o = T(2) - 2 \times u$.
- One solver pass without the loop: $w = T(1) - o$.
- Added per iteration by the loop form: $u - w$.
- The 8 line-search iterations of all 4 solver iterations: $2 \times (T(4) - T_4)$.
