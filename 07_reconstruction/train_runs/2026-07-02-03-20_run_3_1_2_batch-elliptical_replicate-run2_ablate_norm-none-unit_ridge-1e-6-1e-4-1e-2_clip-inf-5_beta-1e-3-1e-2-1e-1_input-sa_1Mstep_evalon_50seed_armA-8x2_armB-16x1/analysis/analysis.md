## Science results (all eval reward, run-2 protocol)

1. **Replication succeeded**: (raw, λ=1e-6, no clip, β=0.01) = **52.76 ± 6.18 (n=33)** vs run-2's
   44.49 ± 3.32 — within about one combined standard error. The run-3.1.1 gap was entirely caused by the
   implementation deltas; environment/trainer/solver are exonerated (the float32-vs-float64 solver probe
   had already shown max relative divergence 3.6e-4).
2. **The clip is the dominant single factor at small ρ**: replica 52.76 → 4.90 with clip=5 alone;
   measured clip-hit fraction 99% (the bonus saturates at the cap for essentially every sample, erasing
   the novelty ranking). Damage tracks measured saturation (99/96/89% at small ρ); at ρ≥1 the clip binds
   1.6% of the time and is inert (17.85 vs 17.33) — the boundedness intuition holds exactly there.
3. **The ridge acts through the measured ratio** $\rho = \lambda d / \overline{\lVert\varphi\rVert^2}$:
   no-clip cells broadly decline as ρ grows (52.8 @ 3.7e-6 → 35.2 @ 3.7e-4 → 26–29 across the 1e-4–1e-2
   decades → 17.3 @ 1.28); top cell = smallest ρ, as pre-registered. The trend is coarse, not strictly
   monotone between neighboring decades (unit/1e-6 at ρ=1.3e-4 scores 26.1, below none/1e-4 at ρ=3.7e-4).
4. **Normalization acts mainly by raising the ρ floor**: at comparable measured ρ, unit ≈ raw
   (26.1 ± 7.8 at ρ=1.3e-4 vs 35.2 ± 5.8 at ρ=3.7e-4, overlapping SEs) — the corner-seeking-bias
   hypothesis is NOT needed. Raw features' measured mean ‖z‖² ≈ 35 gives 35× smaller ρ at equal λ; unit
   norm would need λ ≈ 3e-8 to match run-2's contrast.
5. **Internal consistency**: (unit, 1e-2, clip 5, β=0.01) = 9.58 ± 3.84 (eval) reproduces run-3.1.1's
   9.03 ± 3.22 (train). β⋆ shifts inversely with bonus scale; β=0.1 at tiny ρ collapses to 0.

  and frozen; z(αx) = α·z(x) bit-exact for α ∈ {0, 0.5, 2} on the real network.
- 8 fixes applied from its findings: the corner-bias statement now accounts for velocity (clipped ±5:
  max squared-norm 50 vs position's 56.25 — "corner-seeking" is the position shadow of an extreme-state
  premium); the β-cannot-help argument restated as a signal-to-offset-ratio claim; findings list the
  no-clip ladder in ρ order and NAME the one inversion (unit 26.1 below raw 35.2 at smaller ρ — the one

  replay-buffer draw (lifetime visitation), not recent transitions; all prose numbers re-stated at one
  935-run snapshot matching the committed tables; per-column best/second marks added to the cell table.

## What this analysis will produce

1. **12-cell table** (each cell at its best β): normalization, λ, clip, β⋆, final **eval** extrinsic
   reward $\bar{R} = \frac{1}{n}\sum_i R_i$ with $\mathrm{SE} = s/\sqrt{n}$, a **success rate** over
   seeds (fraction with final eval reward above a threshold chosen from the pooled bimodal gap; Wilson
   interval) with median/IQR — the per-seed outcome is bimodal (run-3.1.1 medians were 0), so mean ± SE
   alone is misleading — plus the training-reward secondary column. NaN-final runs counted per cell,
   never dropped silently.
2. **Reward-over-training figure**: the replica cell (raw, 1e-6, no clip), its single-factor swaps, the
   (unit, 1e-2, 5) run-3.1.1-like cell, and the run-2 `rnd_elliptical` reference — all EVAL reward (no
   metric caveat).
3. **Measured-ρ diagnostics table** per cell from `intrinsic_diagnostics`: mean ‖z‖², mean ‖φ‖²,
   effective ridge ratio $\rho = \lambda d / \overline{\lVert\varphi\rVert^2}$ (measured vs. assumed),
   Λ eigenvalue range, clip-hit fraction — the direct check of the ridge-ratio hypothesis vs. the
   corner-bias hypothesis.
4. **Slurm-shape comparison (arm A = 8 tasks × 2 CPUs vs arm B = 16 tasks × 1 CPU)**: per-run duration
   distributions paired per config on reservation silicon only (jaguar03 / puma01 co-located pairs),
   per-node throughput (runs/node/day), and infrastructure error rate = `failed/` + rc=124 timeouts +
   out-of-memory kills + stale `running/` markers older than 24 h (job-walltime losses classified
   separately from unexpected crashes). Worker logs (claim lines carry jobid + config + duration + rc)
   joined with sacct give per-run node attribution.
