## Science results (all eval reward, run-2 protocol)

1. **Replication succeeded**: (raw, λ=1e-6, no clip, β=0.01) = **52.76 ± 6.18 (n=33)** vs run-2's
   44.49 ± 3.32 — within about one combined standard error. The run-3.1.1 gap was entirely caused by the
   implementation deltas; environment/trainer/solver are exonerated (the float32-vs-float64 solver probe
   had already shown max relative divergence 3.6e-4).
2. **The clip is the dominant single factor at small ρ**: replica 52.76 → 4.90 with clip=5 alone;
   measured clip-hit fraction 99% (the bonus saturates at the cap for essentially every sample, erasing
   the novelty ranking). Damage tracks measured saturation (99/96/89% at small ρ); at ρ≥1 the clip binds
   1.6% of the time and is inert (17.85 vs 17.33) — the boundedness intuition holds exactly there.
3. **The ridge acts through the measured ratio** $\rho = \lambda d / \overline{\lVert\varphi\rVert^2}$:
   no-clip cells broadly decline as ρ grows (52.8 @ 3.7e-6 → 35.2 @ 3.7e-4 → 26–29 across the 1e-4–1e-2
   decades → 17.3 @ 1.28); top cell = smallest ρ, as pre-registered. The trend is coarse, not strictly
   monotone between neighboring decades (unit/1e-6 at ρ=1.3e-4 scores 26.1, below none/1e-4 at ρ=3.7e-4).
4. **Normalization acts mainly by raising the ρ floor**: at comparable measured ρ, unit ≈ raw
   (26.1 ± 7.8 at ρ=1.3e-4 vs 35.2 ± 5.8 at ρ=3.7e-4, overlapping SEs) — the corner-seeking-bias
   hypothesis is NOT needed. Raw features' measured mean ‖z‖² ≈ 35 gives 35× smaller ρ at equal λ; unit
   norm would need λ ≈ 3e-8 to match run-2's contrast.
5. **Internal consistency**: (unit, 1e-2, clip 5, β=0.01) = 9.58 ± 3.84 (eval) reproduces run-3.1.1's
   9.03 ± 3.22 (train). β⋆ shifts inversely with bonus scale; β=0.1 at tiny ρ collapses to 0.

Interim findings:

1. **Unit normalization at a replica-level ridge ratio does NOT recover the replica.** The best beta row
   (beta=1e-3) reaches 22.99 ± 5.75 vs the replica's 53.81 ± 5.95 (n=40, final arm data) — a gap of
   about 3.7 combined standard errors, with the unit cell sitting at an even SMALLER measured $\rho$
   ($1.28\times 10^{-6}$ vs $3.7\times 10^{-6}$).
2. **This revives the raw-feature extreme-state-premium hypothesis (Hypothesis 2).** The ridge ratio
   alone does not carry run-2's advantage: within unit norm, lowering the ridge from 1e-6
   ($\rho = 1.3\times 10^{-4}$, best cell 19.3 ± 5.0 at the final pooled arm data) to 1e-8
   ($\rho = 1.3\times 10^{-6}$, best 22.99 ± 5.75) leaves the reward statistically unchanged (gap
   under one combined standard error) — the unit-norm ladder is flat in $\rho$ — while raw features
   at a comparable $\rho$ score about twice as high. Hypothesis 2 was dropped on
   parsimony in the main grid; this direct test brings it back as the live explanation.
3. **The beta collapse matches finding 5's scaling story.** beta=1e-4 and 1e-3 are statistically tied
   (20.07 ± 6.34 vs 22.99 ± 5.75); beta ≥ 0.1 collapses to ~0 as the large-contrast bonus swamps the
   sparse extrinsic reward.

### Infrastructure event (2026-07-04): srun WaitTime killed 94 mid-flight runs

The sweep's queue drains in ONE wave (256 workers ≥ 250 configs), so each worker exits after its single
run. This cluster's `slurm.conf` sets `WaitTime=3600`, and srun's default is to kill ALL remaining tasks
of a step 3600 s after the FIRST task exits. Consequences, per job: the 3 jobs whose spare workers found
the queue already empty died at exactly 1 h 00 m (42 runs killed at ~55–90k steps); 10 more jobs died
~1 h after their fastest worker finished (12–18 h in); 1 job finished all 16 runs within the hour window
(COMPLETED); 1 job (ai05, gnolim) had no finisher yet at recovery time and keeps running. The
3.1.2 arm sweeps never hit this because their 900-config queues kept every worker busy to the 4-day
walltime.

- **Category**: job-level infrastructure kill (worker SIGKILLed, marker left in `running/`); the model
  never got to finish, so these attempts are excluded from every denominator above and surfaced here.
- **Counts**: 94 killed attempts archived (per the requeue rule) to
  `data/<sweep_id>/killed_attempts_2026-07-04-16-34/`, their markers requeued and picked up by 6
  recovery jobs within a minute; 16 first-attempt runs still in flight on ai05 (its job predates the
  fix, so up to 15 of those 16 may be killed ~1 h after its first finisher; they will be archived and
  requeued the same way when that job ends).
- **No outcome bias**: which runs got killed depends on node speed and job composition (timing), not on
  the runs' rewards, so the completed-so-far subset is unbiased for the per-beta means; the requeued
  configs rerun the same (beta, seed) pairs.
- **Fix**: `worker_16x1.slurm` / `worker_8x2.slurm` now pass `srun --wait=0` (never kill siblings after
  a task exits); recorded in the project rule `.claude/rules/slurm-submission.md` and the global cluster
  rule.
