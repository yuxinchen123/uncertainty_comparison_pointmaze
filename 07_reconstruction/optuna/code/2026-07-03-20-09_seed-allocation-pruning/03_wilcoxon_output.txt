decision rule: WilcoxonPruner prunes a cell when a signed-rank test says it is
statistically worse than the BEST cell so far (relative). The user's rule prunes when a
cell's own 95% upper bound falls below the fixed bar 35.0 (absolute). A cell above
35 but below the best cell (e.g. cell 1 vs cell 0) can be pruned by Wilcoxon yet kept by the
user's absolute rule; that is the key difference.
